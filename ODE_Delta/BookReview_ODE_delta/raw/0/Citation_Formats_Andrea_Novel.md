**Book Details:**
- Title: *Control Theory for Engineers: A Primer*
- Authors: Brigitte d'Andréa-Novel, Michel De Lara
- Publisher: Springer
- Year: 2013
- ISBN: 978-3-642-34323-0
- DOI: 10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7

## CITATION FORMATS BY STYLE

### **1. APA (7th Edition) - RECOMMENDED FOR ENGINEERING/SCIENCE**

**Basic format:**
```
d'Andréa-Novel, B., & De Lara, M. (2013). Control theory for engineers: 
A primer. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7
```

**With retrieved date (for online sources):**
```
d'Andréa-Novel, B., & De Lara, M. (2013). Control theory for engineers: 
A primer. Springer. Retrieved from https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7
```

**Edited chapter reference (if citing a specific chapter):**
```
d'Andréa-Novel, B., & De Lara, M. (2013). Chapter title. In Control theory 
for engineers: A primer (pp. XX-XX). Springer. 
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7
```

## GENERAL GUIDELINES

### **Punctuation of Author Names:**
- **Apostrophe in surname:** d'Andréa-Novel (not d'Andrea-Novel)
- **Hyphenated surname:** Correct as d'Andréa-Novel throughout
- **First author format:** Varies by style (see examples above)

### **Title Capitalization:**
- **Sentence case (APA, Chicago Author-Date):** "Control theory for engineers: a primer"
- **Title case (MLA, Chicago Notes-Bib, Harvard):** "Control Theory for Engineers: A Primer"

### **Publisher Location:**
- For Springer: Publication city often omitted in modern citations
- Full: Springer-Verlag Heidelberg New York Dordrecht London
- Short: Springer (preferred in most modern styles)

### **When Citing Specific Pages:**
- APA: Include page range (pp. XX-XX) after publisher
- Chicago: Include page number after title or in parentheses
- Harvard: Include page range in brackets [pp. XX-XX]

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| d'Andrea-Novel | d'Andréa-Novel (accent on e) |
| Andrea-Novel (without apostrophe) | d'Andréa-Novel |
| Brigitte De Lara | Michel De Lara (separate authors) |
| "Control theory for engineers" (all lowercase) | *Control Theory for Engineers* (capitalized title) |
| Missing DOI | Include: 10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7 |
| Wrong ISBN | 978-3-642-34323-0 (print) not 978-3-642-34324-7 |

## MOST APPROPRIATE FOR ENGINEERING/SCIENCE ARTICLES

### **Primary Recommendation: IEEE Style**
```
[#] B. d'Andréa-Novel and M. De Lara, Control Theory for Engineers: 
    A Primer. Springer, 2013, doi: 10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7.
```
- **Why:** Standard in engineering and control systems
- **Easy to number:** Works with numbered reference lists
- **Includes DOI:** Modern best practice

### **Secondary Recommendation: APA Style**
```
d'Andréa-Novel, B., & De Lara, M. (2013). Control theory for engineers: 
A primer. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7
```
- **Why:** Widely accepted in engineering and control disciplines
- **Author-Date system:** Easy to find in narrative
- **Clear formatting:** Easy to read

### **For Control Systems / Vibration Engineering Papers:**
Use **IEEE style** for consistency with field standards

### **For Multidisciplinary or Mathematical Papers:**
Use **APA or Chicago** for clarity

## EXAMPLE: HOW TO USE IN YOUR LITERATURE REVIEW TEXT

### **Narrative citation (Chicago style):**
```
d'Andréa-Novel and De Lara (2013) provide a comprehensive primer on control 
theory for practicing engineers, with emphasis on practical applications and 
theoretical foundations.
```

### **Parenthetical citation (APA style):**
```
Control theory fundamentals are well-established in engineering practice 
(d'Andréa-Novel & De Lara, 2013).
```

### **Numbered citation (IEEE style):**
```
Fundamental control theory concepts are presented in [15].

[15] B. d'Andréa-Novel and M. De Lara, Control Theory for Engineers: 
     A Primer. Springer, 2013, doi: 10.1007/978-3-642-34324-7.
```
